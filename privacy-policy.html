      Privacy Policy

      Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information is and isn’t collected when you use ToolifyHub.

      Last updated: October 18, 2026

      In short: the tools on ToolifyHub run entirely in your web browser. The text, images and numbers you enter are processed on your device and are not uploaded to, seen by, or stored by us.

      1. Information you enter into tools

      All tools on this website — including the text tools, image tools, developer tools, calculators, QR code generator and password generator — process your input locally using JavaScript in your browser. We do not receive, log or store that content. When you close or refresh the page, it is gone.

      2. Information stored on your device

      ToolifyHub stores one small preference in your browser’s local storage: your choice of light or dark mode (under the key theme). It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time through your browser settings. ToolifyHub itself does not set cookies.

      3. Server and hosting logs

      Like almost every website, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information when pages are requested, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the date and time. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and diagnose problems. These logs do not include anything you type or upload into our tools.

      4. Analytics

      We do not currently use analytics or tracking tools. If we add privacy-friendly analytics in the future, we will update this policy first and describe what is collected.

      5. Advertising

      ToolifyHub does not currently display advertisements. To keep the tools free, we may show ads in the future. If we do, we will update this policy before ads go live, explaining which advertising partners we use, how they use cookies, and how you can opt out.

      6. Emails you send us

      If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use this only to reply to you and improve ToolifyHub, and we don’t sell or share it. You can ask us to delete your correspondence at any time.

      7. Links to other websites

      Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their privacy policies.

      8. Children’s privacy

      ToolifyHub is a general-audience website and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

      9. Your rights

      Depending on where you live — for example in the UK, the European Union, California, Canada or Australia — you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information held about you. Because our tools don’t collect personal data, in most cases we hold none. If you have emailed us, contact us to exercise these rights.

      10. Changes to this policy

      We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be highlighted on this page.

      11. Contact

      Questions about privacy? Email contact@example.com.
