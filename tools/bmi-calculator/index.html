      About the BMI Calculator

      BMI is a quick screening measure used by doctors and health organisations, including the World Health Organization (WHO), to sort adult weight into broad categories. It is a useful starting point, but it doesn’t measure body fat directly or tell the full story of your health.

      How to use the BMI Calculator

      1. Choose metric units (centimetres and kilograms) or US units (feet, inches and pounds).
      2. Enter your height and weight. The result updates as you type.
      3. Read your BMI value and category, and see where it falls on the colour scale.
      4. Check the healthy weight range for your height, and read the health note for context.

      Example use cases

      • Getting a quick, general check of whether your weight is in a typical range for your height.
      • Tracking changes over time alongside other measures such as waist size.
      • Understanding a BMI figure mentioned by a doctor or in a health report.
      • Converting between metric and US units for the same measurement.

      How BMI is calculated

      BMI is your weight in kilograms divided by your height in metres squared: BMI = kg ÷ m². For example, a person who is 1.75 m tall and weighs 70 kg has a BMI of 70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) ≈ 22.9. In US units, the formula is 703 × lb ÷ in², which gives the same result.

      BMICategory (adults, WHO)
      Below 18.5Underweight
      18.5 – 24.9Healthy weight
      25.0 – 29.9Overweight
      30.0 and aboveObesity

      Some health authorities use lower thresholds for people of South Asian, Chinese and some other backgrounds. Your doctor can advise what applies to you.

      Frequently asked questions

      Is BMI accurate?

      BMI is a useful population-level screening tool, but it is an estimate for individuals. It doesn’t account for muscle mass, bone density, body shape or where fat is stored. Very muscular people may have a high BMI without excess fat.

      Can children use this calculator?

      No. For children and teens aged 2–19, BMI must be compared with growth charts for their age and sex. Use a dedicated child BMI percentile calculator from a health authority or ask your doctor.

      What is a healthy BMI?

      For most adults, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered a healthy weight range. Health also depends on diet, activity, genetics and other factors.

      Is my information saved?

      No. Your height and weight are used only for the calculation in your browser. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.

      Is this medical advice?

      No. This calculator provides general information only and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
