      About the Word Counter

      Writers, students and marketers often need to hit a specific length: a 500-word essay, a 150-word abstract, or a two-minute speech. This counter updates as you type, so you can edit with the target in view. Your text is counted on your own device and never leaves your browser.

      How to use the Word Counter

      1. Type directly into the text box, or paste text you have copied from a document, email or web page.
      2. Watch the counts update instantly: words, characters with and without spaces, sentences and paragraphs.
      3. Check the reading and speaking time estimates if you are preparing an article, presentation or video script.
      4. Edit your text in the box until you reach your target length, then use Copy text to take it back to your document.

      Example use cases

      • Checking that an essay, cover letter or assignment meets a minimum or maximum word count.
      • Keeping blog posts, product descriptions and newsletters at a consistent length.
      • Timing a speech, podcast intro or video voice-over before recording.
      • Trimming an abstract or personal statement to a strict application limit.
      • Counting words in translation or copywriting work that is billed per word.

      How words, sentences and reading time are counted

      A word is any run of letters or numbers. Hyphenated words such as “well-known” and contractions such as “don’t” count as one word, and accented and non-Latin letters are supported. A sentence ends with a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark. Paragraphs are separated by a blank line.

      Reading time uses an average silent reading speed of about 238 words per minute, and speaking time uses about 130 words per minute, a typical pace for presentations. Real speeds vary from person to person, so treat both as estimates.

      Frequently asked questions

      Is my text saved or uploaded anywhere?

      No. All counting happens in your browser with JavaScript. Your text is not sent to a server and is not stored. It disappears when you close or refresh the page.

      Why might my count differ slightly from Microsoft Word or Google Docs?

      Every program has its own rules for edge cases such as numbers with decimals, URLs, em dashes without spaces, or symbols on their own. Differences are usually just a few words in a long document.

      Does it count words in languages other than English?

      Yes, for languages that separate words with spaces, such as Spanish, French, German, Russian or Arabic. Languages written without spaces, such as Chinese, Japanese and Thai, cannot be split into words this way, so use the character count for them instead.

      How many pages is my word count?

      As a rough guide, about 500 words fill one single-spaced page and about 250 words fill one double-spaced page in a 12-point font. The exact number depends on the font, margins and formatting.

      Can I use it offline?

      Yes. Once the page has loaded, the counter keeps working without an internet connection.
