      About the Lorem Ipsum Generator

      Lorem Ipsum is pseudo-Latin filler text that designers have used for decades. Because it looks like real text but can’t be read, it lets people judge a layout, font and spacing without being distracted by the words.

      How to use the Lorem Ipsum Generator

      1. Enter how much text you need and choose paragraphs, sentences or words.
      2. Choose whether to begin with the classic “Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet” opening and whether to add HTML <p> tags.
      3. Click Generate text. Each click creates a new random variation.
      4. Copy the text or download it as a .txt or .html file.

      Example use cases

      • Filling website and app mockups before the final copy is written.
      • Testing how a blog template or email layout handles long and short paragraphs.
      • Checking typography: font size, line height and line length.
      • Creating sample content for presentations, wireframes and print layouts.
      • Populating a database or CMS with test entries during development.

      Frequently asked questions

      What does Lorem Ipsum mean?

      It comes from a scrambled passage of “De finibus bonorum et malorum”, a philosophical text written by Cicero in 45 BC. The words have been altered and mixed, so the result has no real meaning.

      Why not just use real text?

      Real text draws attention to what it says. Neutral placeholder text keeps feedback focused on the design. That said, testing with realistic content before launch is always a good idea.

      Is generated Lorem Ipsum free to use?

      Yes. Lorem Ipsum is not copyrighted placeholder text, and you can use what you generate here in personal and commercial projects.

      How much text can I generate?

      Up to 100 paragraphs, 500 sentences or 5,000 words at a time.

      Should I remove Lorem Ipsum before publishing?

      Yes. Placeholder text on a live page looks unfinished and provides no value to visitors or search engines. Replace it with real, helpful content.
