        About the JPG to PNG Converter

        PNG is a lossless format, so it does not add further compression artefacts when you edit and re-save an image. Converting a JPG to PNG is useful when an app or form requires PNG, or before adding text and graphics on top of a photo.

        How to use the JPG to PNG Converter

        1. Click the upload area or drag one or more JPG/JPEG images onto it.
        2. Each image is converted to PNG automatically and appears in the list with its new file size.
        3. Click Download next to an image, or Download all to save every PNG.
        4. Use Clear list to start again with new images.

        Example use cases

        • Meeting upload requirements on websites and apps that only accept PNG files.
        • Preparing a photo for editing so that repeated saves do not degrade it further.
        • Adding images to design tools and documents that work best with PNG.
        • Converting a batch of photos for a presentation or school project.

        Frequently asked questions

        Will converting to PNG improve the image quality?

        No. Converting cannot restore detail that JPG compression already removed. It does stop further quality loss in future edits, because PNG is lossless.

        Why is the PNG file larger than the JPG?

        PNG uses lossless compression, which is less efficient for photos than JPG’s lossy compression. A PNG of a photo is often two to five times larger. If file size matters more, keep the JPG or use the Image Compressor.

        Does the PNG have a transparent background?

        No. JPG images have no transparency, so the PNG keeps the original solid background. Removing a background requires a separate editing step.

        Why did “Download all” only save some files?

        Some browsers ask for permission before a site downloads several files at once. Allow multiple downloads when prompted, or download images one by one.

        Are my photos uploaded?

        No. Conversion uses your browser’s Canvas API, and your images never leave your device.
