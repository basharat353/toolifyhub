      About the Age Calculator

      Age calculations are trickier than they look because months have different lengths and leap years add an extra day. This calculator handles those details using calendar dates, so the result matches how ages are counted on official forms.

      How to use the Age Calculator

      1. Enter the date of birth using the date picker or by typing it.
      2. Leave “Calculate age on” as today, or change it to find the age on a past or future date.
      3. The result appears automatically. Click Calculate age if it doesn’t update.
      4. Review the age in years, months and days, the totals, and the countdown to the next birthday.

      Example use cases

      • Checking eligibility for school entry, sports leagues, pensions or retirement.
      • Finding someone’s exact age on a specific date for forms and applications.
      • Counting down to a birthday or working out how many days old someone is.
      • Calculating the age of a pet, a building or the time since an event.

      Frequently asked questions

      How are leap-year birthdays (29 February) handled?

      In years without 29 February, the next birthday is shown as 28 February. Some countries legally use 1 March instead, so check local rules for official purposes.

      Why is “total hours” approximate?

      The calculator uses dates, not times of birth, and assumes 24 hours per day. It doesn’t account for the exact birth time or daylight saving changes.

      Does it count age the way it’s used in East Asia?

      No. It uses the international system, where age starts at zero at birth and increases on each birthday.

      Is my date of birth stored?

      No. The calculation is done in your browser and nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere.
