        About the PNG to JPG Converter

        PNG files, especially photos and screenshots, can be very large. JPG uses lossy compression that usually makes photos much smaller with little visible difference — ideal for email, websites and uploads.

        How to use the PNG to JPG Converter

        1. Set the JPG quality and the background colour that should replace any transparent areas.
        2. Click the upload area or drag one or more PNG images onto it.
        3. Each image is converted immediately. Changing the quality or background re-converts the list.
        4. Download images one at a time or click Download all.

        Example use cases

        • Shrinking large screenshots and PNG photos before emailing them.
        • Uploading images to sites that only accept JPG files.
        • Reducing image sizes for faster-loading web pages.
        • Converting graphics with transparency onto a solid brand-colour background.

        Frequently asked questions

        What happens to transparent areas?

        JPG does not support transparency, so transparent pixels are filled with the background colour you choose. White is the default.

        What quality should I choose?

        For photos, 80–90% gives a good balance of size and quality. For screenshots with text, use a higher quality (90–100%) to keep text crisp, or keep the image as PNG.

        Is JPG always smaller than PNG?

        Usually for photos, often dramatically. For simple graphics with flat colours, a PNG can sometimes be smaller and sharper than a JPG.

        Are my images private?

        Yes. Conversion happens entirely in your browser, and no images are uploaded or stored.
