        Frequently asked questions

        Are ToolifyHub tools really free?

        Yes. Every tool is free to use without an account, a trial period or usage limits. To help cover running costs, the site may show clearly labelled advertisements, which are kept separate from the tools.

        Do you upload or store my files and text?

        No. All 25 tools run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. Images, documents, text and numbers you enter are processed on your own device and are never sent to our servers.

        Do I need to install anything?

        No. ToolifyHub works in any modern browser, such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS.

        Do the tools work on my phone?

        Yes. Every page is designed to work on small screens, and the image tools can use photos from your phone’s gallery or camera roll.

        Can I use the tools offline?

        Once a tool page has loaded, most tools keep working without an internet connection, because all the processing happens on your device.

        Can I suggest a new tool?

        Absolutely. We add tools based on what people need. Send your idea through our contact page.
